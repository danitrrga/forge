Rules. Only the owner and you speak. Launch every agent in the background, `model` exactly as the start JSON gives it, with the absolute paths of the phase's `context.md` and `decisions.tsv`. A launch returns at once: read its result only after its completion notice. You write `decisions.tsv` only with `fwx decision new` (step 5) and never `research/`. A dead agent is not "no findings": say so, launch it once more. Round one asks the owner's view and has no `Recommended:` line; every later question ends with your recommendation and why (`references/rounds.md`); the owner decides. When he asks what you would do, say it plainly, and his asking gets no row of its own (the decision he then takes is recorded as always).

Context. Read no long file or investigation yourself: a `researcher` returns summary and path. Its `## Unproven` lines are not findings: say them as not checked. After a compaction, redo the playback from `context.md`, `decisions.tsv` and the summaries.

1. **Start.** Run `fwx discuss --json`, with `--phase $ARGUMENTS` when a `target` phase path was given. Exit 1: say in one line the framework is off, go on as plain Claude. `bound.stage` after `discuss`: `fwx:session <bound.phase_dir>`, no conversation. `bound.reentry`: follow `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/discuss/references/reentry.md`. `bound.stage` null with a `phase_dir` is a discuss not yet frozen: continue it, no second phase. An `open_phases` entry with `stage` null: ask in round one whether the message continues it. Exit 2: give its one-line reason and stop.

2. **Gather context** before you classify the request. Read only: the code the message touches and `.fwx/` (`project.md` or `PROJECT.md`, `durable/`, `adr.tsv`); for UI work, also the identity and design documents and the component skill (the `.claude/skills/` one whose description says so). What the repo, the app or Linear can answer is looked up, never asked. No phase, no agent yet.

3. **What does the owner ask?** Unsure: ask in round one.
   - Work on the project: the subproject from the message and `subprojects` (unsure: ask, research waits). Then `fwx phase new <slug> [--subproject S]` and launch two or three `researcher` (`models.researcher.small`): code map, `.fwx/` state, for UI the design documents and component skill. Do not wait. First check: what you understood, the smallest change that meets it, the subproject and why. If the change is one or two obvious files, say so and offer plain Claude instead of a phase; on his yes, wait for the researchers' completion notices (a late one would write into the removed folder and leave a phase with no stage row), then `fwx phase remove <phase>`.
   - Too big for one session: wayfinder mode, `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/discuss/references/reentry.md`.
   - Continue: with no phase bound, `fwx:session`.

4. **Check** at the top of every reply to an owner message, a question too: up to three lines, about 50 words: what you understood; "Noted: ..." per decision; a bare "yes" read aloud ("I read your yes as b: ..."). His question gets an answer and no row. Under it, if needed, a numbered round per `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/discuss/references/rounds.md`, only for his taste or direction or what is unclear: round one with no `Recommended:` line, each later question ending with your `Recommended:` line.

5. **Record.** Record each decision of the turn yourself with `fwx decision new --phase <phase_dir>`. A phase holds a handful of rows: the owner's decisions and the open assumptions, a row only for a decision a later step needs and could not guess; a choice with no alternative, or one the code or `context.md` already says, gets none. A lettered or verdict answer is `chosen` (a verdict's JSON is its `source`); a plain yes to what you noted, `approved`; what you decide unasked, `agent-assumption`, as is whether the work is UI by its nature; an order nobody weighed, `unexamined`: raise it once next round, with the alternative and what each costs. A changed decision is a new row with `--supersedes`. What may last past the phase meets all three: hard to reverse, surprising without context, a real trade-off.

6. **Research.** A fact you can look up goes to a `researcher` without holding the round; a bigger investigation, after his yes, to one on `models.researcher.normal`. A fork that a small throwaway script run inline, writing no file, can settle goes to a `researcher` (`models.researcher.small`) that runs it, never to the owner.

7. **UI with demos.** For a bounded UI decision launch `demo` (`models.demo.normal`) with `verdict_tool` and `verdict_command` as `fwx discuss --json` printed them; when it returns, open the page for the owner the way the user's instructions say, say in one line it is ready, with the demo's one-line pick as your `Recommended:` line when it gave one, and end the turn, never waiting on it; the last line of the turn names the page (its path, or the orca tab it opened), so he sees where to answer. His pasted answer's pick and note go into `Approved UI` of `context.md`. No UI choice is closed in chat. Without `verdict_tool`, hand him each figure the same way; he answers by letter.

8. **Claim check.** When all is said and before the playback, write the draft `context.md`; `decisions.tsv` is already written by step 5. Then launch one `researcher` (`models.researcher.normal`) with their absolute paths: it checks every claim in them against the code and the documents and lists who and what the change touches (callers, documents, other phases, rules, ADRs). Read its summary after its notice. A claim it finds false, unproven or contradicted, and anything the change touches that the draft never named, becomes a numbered question in your next message; what the code settles you correct and say in a `Noted:` line.

9. **Playback and sign-off.** Give the playback per `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/discuss/references/playback.md`. He may correct, want more research, map it as several phases (wayfinder mode, `references/reentry.md`) or sign off; only that ends it.

10. **Demo in the app.** With UI and `verdict_command`, `demo` (`models.demo.normal`) runs it in the app; a UI change goes back to step 7.

11. **Freeze.** Rewrite `context.md` with the sections of `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/templates/phase/context.md`. `Surfaces` lists `ui` when the work is UI by its nature (what a person sees or uses), not its file extension: a typo in a `.tsx` is not, a visible change in a `.ts` is. With `decisions.tsv` complete, run `fwx:session <phase>`, stage still `discuss`. On "cancel": wait for the completion notice of any researcher still running, then `fwx phase remove <phase>`, reply `{status: done, reason: "cancelled", phase_dir}`.
